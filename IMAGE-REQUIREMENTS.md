## Photography Direction

### Style
- **Reference:** Cereal Magazine, Kinfolk, Studio KO architecture photography
- **Lighting:** Natural light only, golden hour preferred
- **Perspective:** Eye level or slightly below, no wide-angle distortion
- **Processing:** Minimal, natural colors, no HDR

### Subjects

**Exteriors:**
- 3/4 angle showing depth
- Include environmental context (fog, trees, hills)
- Wait for good light

**Interiors:**
- Shoot toward windows
- Include one wall for scale
- Show the space, not just details

**Details:**
- 45-degree angle on materials
- Fill frame with texture
- Shallow depth acceptable

**Process (optional):**
- Hands at work
- Tools in context
- Materials before installation
