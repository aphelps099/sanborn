The Project

A century-old Victorian requiring extensive rot repair and period-appropriate restoration. The challenge wasn't just structural — it was preserving the character that made this house worth saving in the first place.

We preserved original millwork while updating structural systems, matching historical details where needed, and ensuring another century of life for this Ferndale landmark.

"James understood the soul of this house. He didn't just fix it — he honored it."
— Sarah Mitchell, Homeowner

Scope of Work

Full exterior restoration and interior structural repair

  • Complete rot repair and foundation stabilization
  • Restoration of original exterior millwork and trim
  • Period-appropriate siding replacement
  • Interior structural reinforcement
  • Preservation of original staircase and details
  • Updated systems while maintaining historical character
