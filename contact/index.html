What to Expect

Initial Conversation

I respond to inquiries within 1-2 business days. We'll schedule a call or meeting to discuss your project, timeline, and whether we're a good fit.

For most projects, I'll visit the site to assess the scope and conditions. This helps me give you an accurate estimate and timeline.
